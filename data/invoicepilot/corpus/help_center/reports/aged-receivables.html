Aged receivables report

Last updated: 2025-06-25

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Shows what each client owes, grouped by how overdue it is: current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, and over 90 days.

Click a client to see their invoices. Export as PDF or CSV, or XLSX on 2.3.0 and later.
