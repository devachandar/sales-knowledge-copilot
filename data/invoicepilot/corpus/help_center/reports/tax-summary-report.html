Tax summary report

Last updated: 2025-08-03

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Shows tax collected on invoices and tax paid on expenses for a date range, by tax rate.

Archived tax rates still appear for the periods they were used.
