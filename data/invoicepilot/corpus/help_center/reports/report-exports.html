Export a report

Last updated: 2026-05-11

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Since 2.3.0, every report can be exported as CSV, XLSX, or PDF from the same report view.

Before 2.3.0, reports exported to PDF and CSV only.
