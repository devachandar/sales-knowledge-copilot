Date picker shows the wrong first day of the week

Last updated: 2026-09-03

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.1

BUG-2310-03 (Low): The date-range picker in the custom report builder always starts weeks on Sunday, ignoring the organization's locale setting.

This is cosmetic. Report data is correct. Type dates directly into the range fields if the calendar is confusing.
