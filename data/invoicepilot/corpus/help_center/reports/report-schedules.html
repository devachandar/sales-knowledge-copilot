Schedule report emails

Last updated: 2026-05-11

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.0+

Available on: Professional, Enterprise

Saved reports can be emailed to stakeholders on a recurring basis: daily, weekly, or monthly.

Open a saved report and click Schedule. Add recipients, including people outside your organization.

Note: Scheduled report emails are available on Professional and Enterprise.
