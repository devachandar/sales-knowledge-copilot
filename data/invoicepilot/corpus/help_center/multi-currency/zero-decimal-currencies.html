Zero-decimal currencies (JPY, KRW, and others)

Last updated: 2025-09-18

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Some currencies, like Japanese yen and Korean won, have no decimal places.

Rounding fix

BUG-2000-01: Invoices in JPY and other zero-decimal currencies rounded totals incorrectly, overcharging customers by up to 1 unit per line item. It affected 2.0.0 and was fixed in 2.1.0.

If you issued invoices in a zero-decimal currency on 2.0.0, review them for 1-unit overcharges per line item.
