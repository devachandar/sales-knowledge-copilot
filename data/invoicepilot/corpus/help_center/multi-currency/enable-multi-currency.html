Enable multi-currency

Last updated: 2025-06-10

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Multi-currency invoicing arrived in 2.0.0. It lets you bill customers in their own currency.

  1. Go to Settings > Currencies.
  2. Turn on Multi-currency.
  3. Add the currencies you need.
  4. Set each customer's currency on their profile.
Note: Existing single-currency organizations are not affected until multi-currency is turned on.
