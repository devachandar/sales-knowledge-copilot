Multi-currency accounts and bank reconciliation

Last updated: 2026-08-27

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.1

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

If your bank feed is in a currency different from your invoices, InvoicePilot must convert amounts before matching.

Known issue: BUG-2310-01 (Critical, Open): Auto-match in bank reconciliation does not convert foreign-currency transactions before comparing amounts, so multi-currency accounts see most transactions left unmatched. Affects 2.3.1.

Workaround

  • Use manual matching for foreign-currency transactions until a fix ships.
  • Base-currency transactions still auto-match normally.
  • Do not bulk-confirm suggested matches on multi-currency accounts; review each one.
