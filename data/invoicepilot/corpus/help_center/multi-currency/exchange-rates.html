Exchange rates

Last updated: 2025-06-18

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

InvoicePilot updates exchange rates once a day from a market data provider. You can also enter a rate by hand on any invoice.

Reports convert foreign amounts to your base currency using the rate saved on each transaction.
