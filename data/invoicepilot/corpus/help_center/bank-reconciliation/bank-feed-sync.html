How often bank feeds sync

Last updated: 2026-05-11

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Since 2.3.0, connected bank feeds sync every 4 hours. Before 2.3.0 they synced once a day.

Click Refresh Feed on any bank account to sync on demand, up to 5 times per day.
