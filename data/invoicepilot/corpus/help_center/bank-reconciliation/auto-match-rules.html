How auto-match decides

Last updated: 2026-06-01

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Auto-match pairs a bank transaction with an invoice or expense when the amount matches exactly and the dates are within 7 days.

If several invoices match, auto-match suggests them instead of matching, and you choose.

On 2.3.1, foreign-currency amounts are not converted before they are compared (BUG-2310-01). Use manual matching for those transactions.
