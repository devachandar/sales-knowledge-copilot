Bank feeds: frequently asked questions

Last updated: 2025-11-04

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

How often do bank feeds update?

Bank feeds sync once a day, usually overnight. Transactions from today will appear tomorrow.

Can I connect a credit card?

Yes. Credit cards connect the same way as bank accounts.

Is my bank login stored?

No. The connection uses a read-only token from your bank. InvoicePilot never sees your password.
