Review and confirm suggested matches

Last updated: 2026-05-20

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Suggested matches show a confidence label: High, Medium, or Low.

  • High: amount, date, and reference all match
  • Medium: amount and date match
  • Low: amount matches only

You can confirm all High matches in one click. Medium and Low matches should be checked one at a time.

Undo a confirmed match from the transaction's history within 30 days.
