Finding your way around

Last updated: 2025-06-12

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Version 2.0.0 introduced a redesigned navigation. The left sidebar holds Dashboard, Sales (Invoices, Estimates, Recurring Invoices, Customers), Purchases (Expenses), Banking, Reports, and Settings.

Organization preferences such as currencies, taxes, invoice templates, and users all live under Settings.
