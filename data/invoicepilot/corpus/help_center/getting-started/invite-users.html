Invite users and assign seats

Last updated: 2026-02-03

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Each person who signs in to InvoicePilot uses one seat. You can invite as many users as you have seats.

  1. Go to Settings > Users.
  2. Click Invite User and enter their email.
  3. Pick a role: Admin, Accountant, Staff, or Read-only.
  4. Click Send Invite. The invite link expires after 7 days.

If you run out of seats, an account owner can buy more from Settings > Subscription > Add Seats.

Note: Custom roles beyond the four built-in roles are available on the Enterprise plan only.
