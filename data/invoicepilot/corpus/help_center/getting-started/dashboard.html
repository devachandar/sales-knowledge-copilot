Understanding the dashboard

Last updated: 2025-06-30

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

The dashboard gives an at-a-glance view of revenue, outstanding invoices, and expense trends.

Widgets

  • Total receivables, split into current and overdue
  • Income and expense chart for the last 12 months
  • Top 5 expense categories
  • Cash flow by bank account

Large accounts

In early 2.0.0 builds, accounts with more than 500 contacts saw a blank dashboard (BUG-2000-02). This was fixed within version 2.0.0.
