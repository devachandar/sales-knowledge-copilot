Create your organization

Last updated: 2025-06-20

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Your organization holds your company profile, base currency, fiscal year, and users.

  1. Sign up at app.invoicepilot.example and verify your email.
  2. Enter your company name, country, and industry.
  3. Choose your base currency. This cannot be changed after your first transaction.
  4. Set your fiscal year start month.
  5. Choose a plan: Starter, Professional, or Enterprise.
Note: Your base currency is the currency your reports are shown in. To bill in other currencies, enable multi-currency later under Settings > Currencies.
