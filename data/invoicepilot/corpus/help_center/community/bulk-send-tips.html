Tip: bulk-sending invoices at month end

Posted by ledger_lucy on 2026-02-11 · Community answer, not reviewed by InvoicePilot staff

We send about 300 invoices at month end. Bulk actions only let you pick 50 at a time, so filter by customer group and do it in batches of 50.

Also, do the payment reminders the same way. Works fine for us.
