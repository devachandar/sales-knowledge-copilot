Does the Python SDK retry on its own?

Posted by py_marco on 2026-08-15 · Community answer, not reviewed by InvoicePilot staff

Yes. client = Client(max_retries=3) and it retries 429, 500 and 503. It won't retry IP-4220 validation errors.
