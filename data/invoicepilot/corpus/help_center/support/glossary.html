Glossary

Last updated: 2025-08-01

TermMeaning
SeatOne user license, billed per year
Base currencyThe currency your reports use
Bank feedA live connection that imports transactions from your bank
Auto-matchAutomatic pairing of bank transactions with invoices or expenses (2.3.0+)
Recurring profileThe schedule behind a recurring invoice
StatementA summary of a customer's invoices, payments, and balance
Patch releaseA release like 2.3.1 that only fixes issues and rolls out automatically
