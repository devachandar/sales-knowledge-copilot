How to report a bug

Last updated: 2026-03-01

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Open a ticket with the steps to reproduce, the version shown under Help > About, and any error code such as IP-4220.

If the bug is already tracked, support links your ticket to its BUG- ID and you get an email when it is fixed.
