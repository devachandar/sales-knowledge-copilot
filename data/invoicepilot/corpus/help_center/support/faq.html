Frequently asked questions

Last updated: 2026-06-01

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Can I bill in more than one currency?

Yes, on every plan. Turn on multi-currency under Settings > Currencies.

Do you support SSO?

Not today. See Single sign-on (SSO) and SAML.

How do I add a tax rate?

Settings > Taxes > New Tax Rate.

Can I get a custom invoice template?

Yes, on Professional and Enterprise. Starter includes 3 standard templates.

Can I raise my API rate limit?

Limits are set by plan: 60, 300, or 1,000 requests per minute. Enterprise customers can ask about a custom limit.

Are added seats prorated?

No. Added seats are billed at the full per-seat price and start their own 12-month term.

How do I export statements for all customers?

There is no bulk statement export in the app. Use the Customer Balance report or API v2.
