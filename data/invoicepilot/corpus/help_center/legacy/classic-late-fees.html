Late fees (Classic)

Last updated: 2025-01-20

Applies to: InvoicePilot 1.9.0

Classic can add a late fee to overdue invoices automatically: a fixed amount or a percentage of the subtotal.

Turn it on under Settings > Late Fees. The fee is added 1 day after the due date.

In 1.8.0 the fee was calculated on the total including tax (BUG-1800-02). Fixed in 1.9.0.
