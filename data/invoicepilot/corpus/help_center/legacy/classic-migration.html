Moving from Classic to 2.0.0

Last updated: 2025-06-12

Applies to: InvoicePilot 1.9.0

  1. Your data moves automatically: clients, invoices, payments, expenses, and estimates.
  2. Late fee settings do not move, because 2.x has no automatic late fees.
  3. Classic plans map to new plans: Solo and Team to Starter or Professional, Business to Enterprise.
  4. API v1 keeps working after the move.
