Attachments (Classic)

Last updated: 2025-02-12

Applies to: InvoicePilot 1.9.0

In 1.9.0 you can attach files up to 10 MB.

Emails with attachments over 5 MB can be delayed up to 2 hours (BUG-1900-01, Won't Fix).
