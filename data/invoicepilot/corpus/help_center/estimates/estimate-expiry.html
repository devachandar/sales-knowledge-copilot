Estimate expiry dates

Last updated: 2025-06-20

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Estimates can have an expiry date. After it passes, the client can no longer accept the estimate from the portal.

You can extend an expired estimate by editing the expiry date and sending it again.
