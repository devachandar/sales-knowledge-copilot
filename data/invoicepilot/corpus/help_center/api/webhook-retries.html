Webhook retries and failures

Last updated: 2026-07-20

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.0+

Available on: Professional, Enterprise

Your endpoint must return a 2xx status within 10 seconds. Anything else counts as a failure.

Retry schedule

Failed deliveries are retried up to 8 times over 24 hours with exponential backoff: after 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours, 8 hours, and 8 hours.

Disabled endpoints

If every delivery to an endpoint fails for 3 days, the endpoint is disabled and admins get an email. Re-enable it under Settings > Developer > Webhooks.

Deliveries can arrive more than once and out of order. Use the event id to ignore duplicates.
