Payments endpoint

Last updated: 2026-01-20

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

MethodPathDoes
GET/v2/paymentsList payments
POST/v2/paymentsRecord a payment against one or more invoices
DELETE/v2/payments/{payment_id}Delete a payment and reopen the invoices

Payments made through the client portal are created automatically and appear here.
