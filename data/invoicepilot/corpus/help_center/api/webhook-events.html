Webhook event types

Last updated: 2026-06-15

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.0+

Available on: Professional, Enterprise

Every event has an id starting with evt_, a type, created_at in UTC, and a data object.

Event typeSent when
invoice.createdAn invoice was created (draft or sent)
invoice.sentAn invoice was emailed or marked as sent
invoice.viewedThe client opened the invoice in the portal
invoice.partially_paidA payment covered part of the invoice
invoice.paidThe invoice balance reached zero
invoice.overdueThe due date passed with a balance left
invoice.voidedThe invoice was voided
payment.createdA payment was recorded, including portal payments
payment.deletedA payment was deleted and its invoices reopened
customer.createdA customer was created
customer.updatedA customer's details changed
estimate.acceptedThe client accepted an estimate
estimate.declinedThe client declined an estimate
recurring_invoice.generatedA recurring profile generated an invoice
bank_transaction.matchedA bank transaction was matched, by auto-match or a person
{
  "id": "evt_1Q2w3E4r",
  "type": "invoice.paid",
  "created_at": "2026-07-02T14:05:11Z",
  "data": {"invoice_id": "inv_7731", "amount_paid": 1500.00, "currency": "EUR"}
}
Note: invoice.paid is not sent for voided invoices. Listen for invoice.voided instead.
