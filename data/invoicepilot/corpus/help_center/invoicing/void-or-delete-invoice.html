Void or delete an invoice

Last updated: 2025-07-08

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Draft invoices can be deleted. Sent invoices cannot be deleted; void them instead so your records stay complete.

  1. Open the invoice.
  2. Click More > Void.
  3. Enter a reason. The reason is kept with the invoice.

Voided invoices stay in reports with a zero balance and show a VOID watermark.
