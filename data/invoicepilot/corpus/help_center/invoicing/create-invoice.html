Create and send an invoice

Last updated: 2026-01-22

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

  1. Go to Sales > Invoices and click New Invoice.
  2. Pick a customer. Their currency and payment terms fill in automatically.
  3. Add line items with quantity, rate, and tax.
  4. Choose a template.
  5. Click Save and Send to email it, or Save as Draft.

Invoice numbers follow your prefix and sequence, set under Settings > Invoices. The default is INV-000001.
