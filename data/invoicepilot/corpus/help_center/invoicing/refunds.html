Refund a payment

Last updated: 2026-02-02

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Refunds are recorded against a payment. The invoice is reopened for the refunded amount.

  1. Open the payment from the invoice.
  2. Click Refund and enter the amount.
  3. Choose whether to issue a credit note instead of reopening the invoice.

Refunds of portal card payments go back to the client's card within 5 to 10 business days.
