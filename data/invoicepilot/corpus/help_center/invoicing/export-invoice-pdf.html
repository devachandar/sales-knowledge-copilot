Export invoices as PDF

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Open an invoice and click PDF > Download, or select several and use the bulk Export as PDF action.

Known issue: In 2.3.1, invoices with more than 25 line items export as a single page and drop the remaining lines (BUG-2310-02). See Line items and long invoices for a workaround.
