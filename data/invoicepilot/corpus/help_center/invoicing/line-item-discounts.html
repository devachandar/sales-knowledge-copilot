Discounts on line items

Last updated: 2025-06-18

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Each line item can have a discount as a percentage or a fixed amount. Discounts are applied before tax.

To discount the whole invoice, add a discount on every line or add a negative line item.
