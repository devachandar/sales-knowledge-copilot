Line items and long invoices

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.1

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

An invoice can have up to 500 line items. Each line has an item, description, quantity, rate, discount, and tax.

Long invoices in PDF

Known issue: BUG-2310-02 (High, Open): Invoices with more than 25 line items export as a single-page PDF, silently dropping remaining line items. Affects 2.3.1.

Workaround

  • Send the invoice by email link instead of attaching the PDF. The online view shows every line.
  • Or group related lines so the invoice has 25 line items or fewer.
  • The client portal view is not affected.
