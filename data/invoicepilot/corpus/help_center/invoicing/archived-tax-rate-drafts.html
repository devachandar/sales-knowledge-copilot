Troubleshooting: tax missing on an old draft

Last updated: 2025-09-17

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.1

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Draft invoices that used a tax rate archived later showed an empty tax field instead of the archived rate.

This is BUG-2010-01 (Low). It affected 2.0.1. It was fixed in 2.1.0.

What to do

  • Open the draft and pick the tax rate again.
  • Fixed in 2.1.0.
