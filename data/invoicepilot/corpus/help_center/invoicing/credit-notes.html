Credit notes

Last updated: 2026-02-02

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

A credit note reduces what a client owes without deleting or voiding an invoice. Credit note numbers start with CN-.

  • Apply a credit note to one or more open invoices
  • Or keep it as client credit for future invoices
  • Credit notes appear on customer statements

Credit notes were added in 2.2.0 and are available on every plan.
