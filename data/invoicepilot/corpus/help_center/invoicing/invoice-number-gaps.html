Troubleshooting: gaps in invoice numbers

Last updated: 2025-07-16

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Deleting a draft invoice consumed its number, leaving a gap in the INV- sequence.

This is BUG-2000-04 (Low). It affected 2.0.0. It was fixed in 2.0.1.

What to do

  • Gaps created on 2.0.0 stay in the sequence; numbers are never reused.
  • Since 2.0.1, deleting a draft frees its number.
  • For auditors, export the Invoice Register report, which lists deleted drafts.
