Invoice templates

Last updated: 2026-03-15

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Templates control how your invoice PDF looks: layout, fonts, logo, and which fields appear.

PlanTemplates
Starter3 standard templates
ProfessionalStandard templates plus custom templates
EnterpriseStandard templates plus custom templates

Creating a custom template

  1. Go to Settings > Templates > Invoices.
  2. Click New Custom Template and start from a standard one.
  3. Edit the header, columns, footer, and colors.
  4. Save and set it as default if you like.

Since 2.0.0, invoice PDFs with custom templates render about 2x faster than before.

Note: Starter customers asking for a custom template need to move to Professional or Enterprise.
