Add a new tax rate

Last updated: 2025-08-02

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

  1. Go to Settings > Taxes.
  2. Click New Tax Rate.
  3. Enter a name (for example, NY Sales Tax) and a percentage.
  4. Optionally group rates into a tax group, like state plus county.
  5. Save. The rate is now available on invoice and estimate line items.

Changing a tax rate only affects new invoices. Existing invoices keep the rate they were created with.
