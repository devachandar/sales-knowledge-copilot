Bulk invoice actions

Last updated: 2026-01-21

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

From the Invoices list you can select several invoices and act on all of them at once.

Supported actions

  • Send
  • Send payment reminder
  • Void
  • Mark as sent
  • Export as PDF (zipped)

Limits

You can select up to 100 invoices per bulk action. To act on more, filter the list and repeat.

Bulk actions were added in version 2.2.0.
