Payment reminders

Last updated: 2025-09-20

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Version 2.1.0 added client-level payment reminders with configurable send windows.

  1. Go to Settings > Reminders to set defaults: for example 3 days before due, on the due date, and 7 days after.
  2. Pick a send window, such as weekdays 9:00 to 17:00 in the client's timezone.
  3. Override per client from the customer's page.

Since 2.2.0 you can also send reminders in bulk from the Invoices list.
