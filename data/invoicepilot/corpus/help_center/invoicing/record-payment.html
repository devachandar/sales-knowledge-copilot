Record a payment

Last updated: 2026-01-22

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Record payments you receive outside the client portal, such as bank transfers or checks.

  1. Open the invoice and click Record Payment.
  2. Enter the amount, date, and method.
  3. Click Save. The invoice status changes to Paid or Partially paid.

Payments made through the client portal are recorded automatically.
