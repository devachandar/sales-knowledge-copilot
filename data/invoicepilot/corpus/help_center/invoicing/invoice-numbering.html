Invoice numbering

Last updated: 2025-07-16

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Invoices are numbered INV-000001, INV-000002, and so on. You can change the prefix and the next number under Settings > Invoices.

Numbers are never reused, even if an invoice is voided.

On 2.0.0, deleting a draft left a gap in the sequence (BUG-2000-04). Since 2.0.1, deleting a draft frees its number.
