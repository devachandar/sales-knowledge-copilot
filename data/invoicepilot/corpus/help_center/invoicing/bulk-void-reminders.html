Troubleshooting: reminders sent for voided invoices

Last updated: 2026-03-11

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Invoices voided with a bulk action kept their scheduled payment reminders, so clients got reminders for void invoices.

This is BUG-2200-03 (Medium). It affected 2.2.0. It was fixed in 2.2.1.

What to do

  • On 2.2.0, cancel reminders by hand after a bulk void: open each client > Reminders.
  • Since 2.2.1, a bulk void cancels reminders automatically.
