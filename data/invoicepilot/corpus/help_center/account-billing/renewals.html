Renewals and license expiry

Last updated: 2026-03-02

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Every license runs for 12 months from its order date. You can see each license's expiry date under Settings > Subscription.

Licenses renew for another 12 months when you place a Renewal order. We email the account owner 60, 30, and 7 days before expiry.

If a license expires, users on those seats lose sign-in access. Data is kept for 90 days.
