Single sign-on (SSO) and SAML

Last updated: 2026-04-02

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

InvoicePilot does not support SSO or SAML sign-in on any plan today.

Users sign in with email and password, and you can require two-factor authentication for everyone under Settings > Security.

If SSO is important to you, tell your account manager so your request is counted.
