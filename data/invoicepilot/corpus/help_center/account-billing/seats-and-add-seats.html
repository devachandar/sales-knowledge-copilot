Seats and adding seats

Last updated: 2026-03-02

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

InvoicePilot is billed per seat per year. One seat lets one user sign in.

Adding seats

Account owners can add seats from Settings > Subscription > Add Seats. Added seats are billed at the plan's full per-seat price and start their own 12-month term from the day you add them. They are not prorated.

Removing seats

Seats can be reduced at renewal. Unused seats are not refunded mid-term.
