Client Portal 2.0 overview

Last updated: 2026-01-20

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Version 2.2.0 introduced Client Portal 2.0, a single self-serve page where your clients can:

  • View their full invoice history
  • Download statements
  • Pay online
  • Accept or decline estimates

Existing portal links from earlier versions keep working with no changes.
