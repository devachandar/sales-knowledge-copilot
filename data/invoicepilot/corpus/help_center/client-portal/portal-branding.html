Brand your client portal

Last updated: 2026-01-25

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0+

Available on: Professional, Enterprise

Add your logo and accent color so the portal matches your brand.

Go to Settings > Client Portal > Branding.

Note: Portal branding is available on Professional and Enterprise. Starter portals use the default InvoicePilot look.
