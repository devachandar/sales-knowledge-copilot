Troubleshooting: client portal login loop on Safari

Last updated: 2026-01-28

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

BUG-2200-01: Safari users were redirected back to the login page after successful authentication due to a cookie SameSite misconfiguration.

This affected 2.2.0 and was fixed in a hotfix within 2.2.0. No action needed.

If a client still sees the loop, ask them to clear cookies for portal.invoicepilot.example and sign in again.
