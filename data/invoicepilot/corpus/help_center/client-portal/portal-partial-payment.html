Troubleshooting: client can't pay the rest of an invoice

Last updated: 2026-05-12

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.1

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Clients could not pay the rest of a partially paid invoice from the client portal because the Pay now button was hidden.

This is BUG-2210-01 (High). It affected 2.2.1. It was fixed in 2.3.0.

What to do

  • On 2.2.1, send the client a payment link from the invoice: More > Copy payment link.
  • Fixed in 2.3.0.
