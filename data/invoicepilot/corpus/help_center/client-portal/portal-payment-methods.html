Payment methods in the client portal

Last updated: 2026-01-22

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Clients can pay by card or bank transfer (ACH in the US, SEPA in the EU).

Card payments are settled to your bank account in 2 business days. Bank transfers take 3 to 5 business days.

Portal payments are created as payments automatically and trigger the payment.created webhook.
