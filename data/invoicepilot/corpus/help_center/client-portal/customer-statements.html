Customer statements

Last updated: 2026-02-10

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.2.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

A statement lists a customer's invoices, payments, and balance for a date range.

For one customer

Open the customer and click Statement. Download as PDF or email it. Clients can also download their own statements from the portal.

For many customers

There is no bulk statement export in the app today. Two options:

  • Use the custom report builder (2.3.0+) with the Customer Balance report and export to XLSX.
  • Use API v2: GET /v2/customers/{customer_id}/statements for each customer.
