Troubleshooting: a recurring invoice skipped a cycle

Last updated: 2026-08-15

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Cause: BUG-2100-02, "Recurring invoices skip a cycle across DST changes". Recurring invoice schedules calculated in local time skipped a billing cycle when daylight-saving time shifted, causing missed invoices for some customers.

Affected versions: 2.1.0 through 2.3.0. Fixed in 2.3.1.

If you are on 2.3.1

The fix is already applied. To recover a missed invoice from before the fix, open the recurring profile and click Create Missed Invoice.

If you are on an older version

Upgrade to 2.3.x. As a stopgap, avoid schedules that send between 01:00 and 03:00 local time on DST change days.
