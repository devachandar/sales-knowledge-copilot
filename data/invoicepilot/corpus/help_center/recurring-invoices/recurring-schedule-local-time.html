Recurring invoices use your local time zone

Last updated: 2025-10-12

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.0

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Recurring invoice schedules are calculated in your organization's local time zone, set under Settings > Organization.

If you change your organization's time zone, future send dates are recalculated.

We are aware that some customers have reported a missed cycle around daylight-saving changes. We are investigating.
