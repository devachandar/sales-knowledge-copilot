Recurring cadences explained

Last updated: 2025-10-01

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

CadenceExample
DailyEvery day at 06:00
WeeklyEvery Monday
MonthlyThe 1st of every month, or the last day of the month
CustomEvery 2 weeks, every 3 months, or every N days

If a monthly schedule falls on a day that does not exist, like February 30, the invoice goes out on the last day of that month.
