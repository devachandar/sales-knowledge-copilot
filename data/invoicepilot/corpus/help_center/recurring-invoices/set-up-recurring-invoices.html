Set up recurring invoices

Last updated: 2025-09-17

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Recurring invoices were added in 2.1.0. They generate and send an invoice on a schedule.

  1. Go to Sales > Recurring Invoices > New.
  2. Choose the customer and line items.
  3. Pick a cadence: daily, weekly, monthly, or custom.
  4. Set a start date and an optional end date.
  5. Choose whether to send automatically or save as draft for review.

Before you confirm, the preview shows the next 3 scheduled send dates.
