Troubleshooting: recurring preview shows the wrong currency

Last updated: 2026-01-21

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.1

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

The preview of upcoming recurring invoices showed the base currency symbol for clients billed in another currency. The invoices themselves were correct.

This is BUG-2110-01 (Low). It affected 2.1.1. It was fixed in 2.2.0.

What to do

  • Only the preview was wrong. Generated invoices used the right currency.
  • Fixed in 2.2.0.
