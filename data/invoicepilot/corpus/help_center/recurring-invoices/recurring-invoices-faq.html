Recurring invoices: frequently asked questions

Last updated: 2026-08-20

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.1+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Which time zone do schedules use?

Since 2.3.1, UTC internally. Dates are shown in your local time.

Can a recurring invoice charge a card automatically?

No. Clients pay from the invoice email or the portal.

Can I change the amount for one cycle?

Yes. Edit the generated draft before it is sent, or set the profile to create drafts.

What happens on the 31st in shorter months?

The invoice goes out on the last day of the month.
