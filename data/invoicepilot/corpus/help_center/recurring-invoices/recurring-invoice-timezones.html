How recurring invoice schedules handle time zones

Last updated: 2026-08-14

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.3.1+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Starting in 2.3.1, recurring invoice schedules are calculated in UTC internally. Send times are still shown in your organization's local time zone.

This change fixed BUG-2100-02, where schedules calculated in local time skipped a cycle when daylight-saving time shifted.

No action is needed. Existing schedules were migrated automatically when 2.3.1 rolled out.
