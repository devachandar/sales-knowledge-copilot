Scan receipts

Last updated: 2025-10-02

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Added in 2.1.0. Take a photo of a receipt in the mobile app, or upload one, and InvoicePilot extracts the vendor, date, and amount.

Accuracy

Typed and printed receipts scan reliably. Always check the extracted amount before saving.

Known issue: BUG-2100-01 (Won't Fix): OCR accuracy on handwritten receipts is below 40%; typed/printed receipts are unaffected. Documented as a known limitation.
