Troubleshooting: scanned receipt is sideways

Last updated: 2025-11-04

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.0

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Receipts photographed in landscape were rotated 90 degrees before OCR, so the amount was often missed.

This is BUG-2100-03 (Low). It affected 2.1.0. It was fixed in 2.1.1.

What to do

  • Retake the photo in portrait mode, or update to 2.1.1.
  • You can always type the amount by hand.
