Mileage expenses

Last updated: 2025-09-17

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Record mileage as an expense: enter the distance and InvoicePilot multiplies it by your mileage rate.

Set the rate under Settings > Expenses. Rates can be per mile or per kilometer.
