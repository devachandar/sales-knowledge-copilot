Record an expense

Last updated: 2025-07-01

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.0.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

  1. Go to Purchases > Expenses > New.
  2. Enter date, amount, vendor, and category.
  3. Optionally mark it billable and link it to a customer.
  4. Attach a receipt image or PDF.

Since 2.1.0 you can bulk-reassign expense categories from the expenses list.
