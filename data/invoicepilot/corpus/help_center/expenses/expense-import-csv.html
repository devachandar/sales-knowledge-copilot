Import expenses from CSV

Last updated: 2026-03-11

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.0+

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Go to Expenses > Import and upload a CSV with date, vendor, amount, currency, and category columns.

Since 2.2.1, dates can be YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY. Before that, only YYYY-MM-DD worked (BUG-2110-02).

Up to 2,000 rows per file.
