Troubleshooting: expense CSV import fails on dates

Last updated: 2026-03-11

Applies to: InvoicePilot 2.1.1

Available on: Starter, Professional, Enterprise

Importing expenses from CSV failed for files with dates written as DD/MM/YYYY.

This is BUG-2110-02 (Medium). It affected 2.1.1. It was fixed in 2.2.1.

What to do

  • On 2.1.1 to 2.2.0, convert dates to YYYY-MM-DD before importing.
  • Since 2.2.1, DD/MM/YYYY is accepted.
