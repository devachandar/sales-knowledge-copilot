# Runbook: escalating a Critical bug

1. Support tags the ticket critical and links the BUG- ID.
2. On-call engineer is paged within 15 minutes.
3. Enterprise customers get an update every 4 hours until resolved; this matches the 24/7 handling promised for Critical issues.
4. Post a status page incident if more than 50 organizations are affected.
